# Topical Map — sprayfoamscience.com

## Editorial angle (decided from real search data, see keywords.json)
This is NOT a generic "how spray foam works" explainer site. DataForSEO PAA data surfaced a large,
current "spray foam has become a red flag" consumer concern cluster (hard to sell a house with it,
insurance companies skeptical, mold behind it, people removing it) sitting alongside real
foundational search volume on open-cell/closed-cell comparison, R-value, and DIY-kit terms. No
competitor (SPFA, manufacturer sites, trade publications) honestly addresses that reputation cluster
— it's adjacent to their own product liability. That's the differentiation gap this site fills.

**Tone: sober, referee-style building science — not a sales brochure, not fear-mongering.** Failure
modes are explained as root-cause engineering (off-ratio mixing, poor substrate prep, moisture
trapping from bad application) rather than "spray foam is dangerous" clickbait. Every claim traces
to real building-science mechanics, never a fabricated statistic or invented citation.

## Conversion framing
Primary CTA is educational, not transactional: "Read the science" / "See the next topic," not
"Get a Quote." Secondary, soft CTA throughout: "Find a CCA-network insured installer" — positioned
as a trust resource (the site's whole argument is that installation quality is what separates a
good spray foam job from a "red flag" one, so a vetted-installer referral is a natural, earned CTA,
not a bolted-on sales pitch). No hard lead-gen forms dominate the page; one lightweight
"Find an Installer" contact form exists but is not the page's primary visual weight anywhere.
