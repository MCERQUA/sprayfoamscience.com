# Topical Map — sprayfoamscience.com

## Positioning
An authoritative, independently-run science/education hub about spray foam insulation — chemistry,
performance, and (critically, per real PAA data) the honest reasons spray foam has developed a
reputation problem. NOT a contractor's own lead-gen page, NOT insurance-framed (zero insurance
language on-site), similar in spirit to roofmetaverse.com. Soft-funnels to CCA's certified-installer
/ insurance-adjacent network via "Find a Vetted Installer" CTAs, never a hard sell.

## Why this angle over a generic "how spray foam works" explainer
Live DataForSEO PAA pulls (2026-08-20, keywords.json) on the three highest-relevance head terms
surfaced a large, current, and NOT manufacturer-servable question cluster: "why is spray foam
insulation now a red flag," "why is it hard to sell a house with spray foam insulation," "why do
insurance companies not like spray foam insulation," "why are people removing spray foam," "can
mold grow behind spray foam insulation." No manufacturer or trade-association site can honestly
own this territory (it's adjacent to their own product liability). An independent, technically
rigorous, CCA-backed site can — and it connects naturally to CCA's installer-network / insurance
business without the site itself reading as insurance content. This is the "more specific" angle
the data pointed to instead of a generic chemistry explainer.
