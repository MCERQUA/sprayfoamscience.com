## create_design_system designMd

Spray Foam Science is Contractors Choice Agency's own technical building-science education hub about spray foam insulation chemistry, thermal performance, and code compliance. This is NOT a contractor sales page and NOT an insurance page. It reads as an authoritative technical publication, similar in spirit to a building-science institute resource. Palette is strict warm earth tones only: primary rust or terracotta hex A85A2E, dark inverted contrast band deep clay hex 5C3620, accent gold hex C79A4B, secondary bronze hex 8C6239, natural sage green accent hex 64784A, base background warm off white hex FAF7F1, tinted band warm sand hex EFE4D4, body text charcoal hex 2E2419. Absolutely zero purple, pink, or blue anywhere, and zero emoji as icons. Light and bright overall with dark bands used only as punctuation between sections, never dominant. Typography should read confident and technical, editorial rather than salesy. Visual motif is technical building science: clean diagrams, cross section style illustrations of wall assemblies, real spray foam application job site photography as secondary imagery. Avoid chemistry lab cliches such as beakers or lab coats, since this is construction building science, not a chemistry lab. Section rhythm rule: no two adjacent sections may share the same background treatment, alternate base, tinted band, inverted dark band, and full bleed photo band with a scrim. Any hero with text over a photo must keep the darkening scrim at fifty percent opacity or darker exactly where the headline copy sits. Trust signals should be framed around technical accuracy and Contractors Choice Agency's real backing, never fabricated citations, statistics, or customer quotes.
